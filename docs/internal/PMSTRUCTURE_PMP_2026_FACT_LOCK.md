## Verified Facts

### PMP 2026 Date

The updated PMP exam launches on 9 July 2026.

Candidates planning to take the current PMP exam version need to sit before 8 July 2026.

### Current vs Updated Exam

Use this distinction:

- Current exam: version available before 8 July 2026
- Updated exam: version from 9 July 2026 onward

### Updated PMP Exam

- 180 total questions
- 170 scored questions
- 10 unscored pretest questions
- 240 minutes
- People: 33%
- Process: 41%
- Business Environment: 26%

### Current PMP Exam

- 180 questions
- 230 minutes
- 175 scored questions
- 5 unscored pretest questions
- People: 42%
- Process: 50%
- Business Environment: 8%

### Training Wording

Use careful wording.

Current PMI public page uses:

35 hours of project management education/training

The 2026 ECO uses:

at least 35 hours of commercial training in project management aligned with the PMP ECO

Do not use:

35 PDUs

for PMP eligibility.

### Training Caution

Books and practice exams alone are not accepted as eligible commercial training.

### Claim Safety

Avoid misleading claims and guaranteed exam-success language.

Do not claim PM Structure is PMI-approved, official, authorized, an ATP, or a guaranteed-pass provider unless formally verified and approved.

## Safe Public Wording

Use:

- PMP 2026 readiness support
- structured PMP preparation pathway
- independent exam-prep and project management learning platform
- 35 hours of project management education/training where applicable
- 35 hours of commercial training in project management aligned with the PMP ECO where applicable
- official exam fees are excluded unless a specific offer clearly states otherwise

Do not use:

- 35 PDUs for eligibility
- guaranteed pass
- PMI-approved
- official PMI course
- authorized training partner
- PM Structure awards PMP
