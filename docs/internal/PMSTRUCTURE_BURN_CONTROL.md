# PM Structure — Internal Burn-Rate Control

## Purpose

This document controls PM Structure's monthly burn before the business proves repeatable revenue.

PM Structure is currently prioritizing the PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway as the first flagship offer. Until that offer proves demand, delivery quality, and conversion, burn must stay controlled.

This is an internal business-control document. Do not publish it on the public website.

---

## Current Rule

PM Structure should not behave like a full platform before one paid funnel is proven.

The business should first prove:

* one clear flagship offer,
* qualified lead flow,
* paid learner conversion,
* delivery completion,
* real learner feedback,
* one repeatable sales script,
* and at least one corporate lead.

Until then, monthly spending must be reviewed before adding new retainers, paid ads, tools, or team members.

---

## Monthly Burn Thresholds

|        Monthly Burn | Status | Meaning         | Required Action                                                                                                  |
| ------------------: | ------ | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|       0–499,999 PKR | Green  | Controlled burn | Continue only essential spending.                                                                                |
| 500,000–999,999 PKR | Yellow | Burn creep risk | Freeze new spending unless directly tied to revenue. Review every cost line.                                     |
|      1,000,000+ PKR | Red    | High-risk burn  | Stop new spending. Cut or pause non-essential tools, retainers, ads, and contractors until revenue proof exists. |

---

## Revenue-Proof Rule

Do not add significant new cost unless at least one of the following is true:

1. PM Structure has reached at least **$1,500/month** in real collected revenue.
2. PM Structure has at least **3 paid learners in the current month**.
3. PM Structure has at least **10 qualified leads in the current month** and a scheduled conversion process.
4. The cost directly supports a paid cohort that is already sold or invoiced.
5. The owner explicitly approves the cost as strategic and records the reason.

---

## Spending Freeze Rule

If monthly burn enters Yellow status, pause:

* paid ads,
* new software subscriptions,
* new design tools,
* new automation tools,
* extra freelancers,
* non-essential video production,
* non-critical website features,
* new certification pathway expansion,
* resource-store expansion,
* and general brand-building spend with no conversion path.

Continue only:

* website stability,
* payment flow,
* lead forms,
* PMP 2026 funnel,
* compliance fixes,
* analytics/conversion tracking,
* customer delivery,
* and revenue-critical operations.

---

## Red Status Rule

If monthly burn reaches or exceeds **1,000,000 PKR/month**, PM Structure must stop expansion.

Actions required:

1. Freeze new commitments.
2. Review every active cost.
3. Cancel or pause unused software.
4. Pause paid marketing unless it is already profitable.
5. Pause non-PMP course expansion.
6. Pause resource-store expansion.
7. Pause membership feature expansion.
8. Focus only on revenue, delivery, and trust fixes.
9. Recheck conversion metrics weekly.
10. Do not add headcount without direct revenue justification.

---

## Monthly Review Questions

Use these questions every month:

1. What was total collected revenue this month?
2. What was total committed spend this month?
3. What was actual paid spend this month?
4. What was the burn status: Green, Yellow, or Red?
5. Which cost directly created or protected revenue?
6. Which cost did not create revenue?
7. Which software/tool was unused or underused?
8. Which contractor/freelancer task was essential?
9. Which planned spend should be delayed?
10. Is the PMP 2026 funnel producing leads?
11. Is the PMP 2026 funnel producing paid learners?
12. Are we scaling before proof?
13. What must be paused next month?

---

## 90-Day Proof Targets

Before expanding PM Structure as a broad platform, the business should aim to prove:

| Metric                            |                     Target |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------: |
| Paid learners                     |                         20 |
| Real testimonials with permission |                          5 |
| Corporate lead                    |                          1 |
| Repeatable sales script           |                          1 |
| Clear primary funnel              |                          1 |
| Flagship offer                    | PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway |

---

## Decision Rule

If the business has not reached the proof targets, do not expand into additional certification pathways, public membership promises, resource-store launches, or broad paid advertising.

The priority remains:

1. PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway.
2. Compliance-safe claims.
3. Lead capture.
4. Conversion tracking.
5. Paid learner delivery.
6. Verified trust proof.
7. Repeatable sales process.
