# PM Structure — Kill / Pivot Thresholds

## Purpose

This document defines when PM Structure must pause, pivot, or stop expansion if the first flagship offer does not show enough proof.

PM Structure is currently prioritizing the **PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway** as the first flagship offer.

The business should not continue expanding into multiple certifications, broad membership promises, public resource-store launches, paid ads, retainers, or platform features unless the flagship offer proves demand, conversion, delivery quality, and trust.

This is an internal execution-control document. Do not publish it on the public website.

Related execution document: PMSTRUCTURE_90_DAY_MARKETING_SCHEDULE.md defines the marketing actions that should be reviewed against the 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day thresholds.

---

## Core Rule

PM Structure should not keep spending just because the website is built.

The business must prove:

* qualified lead demand,
* paid learner conversion,
* delivery completion,
* real learner feedback,
* one repeatable sales process,
* and at least one corporate lead signal.

If those signals do not appear within the defined timeframes, the business must pause expansion and fix the funnel.

## Primary Kill / Pivot Thresholds

| Timeframe | Trigger                            | Required Action                                                                                        |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 30 days   | Fewer than 10 qualified leads      | Rework traffic source, landing page, CTA, offer angle, or lead form. Do not add paid ads or retainers. |
| 30 days   | Fewer than 3 paid learners         | Rework offer, pricing, follow-up, sales script, or trust proof. Do not expand courses.                 |
| 60 days   | Fewer than 8 paid learners         | Pause secondary certification expansion and diagnose conversion weakness.                              |
| 60 days   | No clear lead source               | Stop broad content expansion and identify one serious acquisition channel.                             |
| 90 days   | Fewer than 20 paid learners        | Pause expansion and rework the PMP 2026 funnel before adding new pathways.                             |
| 90 days   | Fewer than 5 verified testimonials | Do not publish strong trust claims. Improve delivery, feedback collection, and permission workflow.    |
| 90 days   | No corporate lead                  | Rework B2B positioning, corporate offer, outreach list, and proposal.                                  |
| 90 days   | No repeatable sales script         | Do not hire sales/marketing support yet. Document and test the founder-led sales process first.        |

## 30-Day Pivot Rule

After 30 days, review:

| Metric                              | Minimum Signal |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------: |
| Qualified leads                     |             10 |
| Paid learners                       |              3 |
| Sales calls / serious conversations |              5 |
| Working lead form                   |            Yes |
| Working follow-up process           |            Yes |
| One clear public CTA                |            Yes |

If PM Structure does not reach at least **10 qualified leads** or **3 paid learners** after 30 days, the business should not add new spend.

Required action:

1. Rework the homepage CTA.
2. Rework the PMP 2026 landing page.
3. Review the offer name and promise.
4. Review pricing and regional affordability.
5. Check whether the form works.
6. Check whether leads are going to the right destination.
7. Review follow-up speed and message quality.
8. Review traffic source.
9. Review trust proof.
10. Pause new retainers, paid ads, and secondary certification expansion.

Required action:

2. Diagnose pricing friction.
3. Review sales-call objections.
4. Review landing-page clarity.
5. Review checkout or payment friction.
6. Review learner onboarding.
7. Ask early learners what almost stopped them.

10. Pause public membership feature expansion.

## 90-Day Kill / Rework Rule

After 90 days, review:

| Metric                                | Target |
| ------------------------------------- | -----: |
| Paid learners                         |     20 |
| Verified testimonials with permission |      5 |
| Corporate lead                        |      1 |
| Repeatable sales script               |      1 |

If PM Structure has fewer than **20 paid learners** after 90 days, pause expansion.

Do not expand into:

* PRINCE2 public cohort launch,
* Lean Six Sigma public cohort launch,
* PMI-RMP public cohort launch,
* broad resource-store launch,
* aggressive paid ads,
* monthly marketing retainers,
* large content production,
* community-heavy promises,
* or full platform positioning.

Required action:

1. Rework the flagship offer.
2. Rework the traffic source.
3. Rework the lead form.
4. Rework the sales script.
5. Rework regional pricing.
6. Rework trust proof.
7. Rework landing-page structure.
8. Interview qualified leads who did not buy.
9. Interview paid learners.
10. Decide whether to continue, narrow, or pivot.

## Stop-Spend Triggers

Stop or pause new spending immediately if any of these happen:

1. Monthly burn reaches Yellow or Red status under the burn-control document.
2. Paid ads generate clicks but no qualified leads.
3. Lead forms receive submissions but no serious conversations.
4. Sales calls happen but no one buys.
5. Buyers object mainly to trust, legitimacy, or unclear certification authority.
6. Buyers object mainly to pricing and no regional pricing exists.
7. The site continues to promote non-live products as purchasable.
8. The business is adding features before fixing conversion.
9. New tools are being added without revenue proof.
10. Contractors are working on non-revenue tasks during pre-proof stage.

---

## What Must Not Happen

Do not keep building because the website looks incomplete.

Do not expand because competitors have more pages.

Do not launch more certification pages before PMP 2026 converts.

Do not spend on paid ads before conversion tracking works.

Do not sell membership as the main product before active learner value exists.

Do not publish fake testimonials or unverified numbers.

Do not overclaim official certification status.

Do not promise guaranteed pass, jobs, promotions, salary increases, or visa outcomes.

Do not confuse "activity" with proof.

## Funnel Diagnosis Questions

Use these questions when a threshold is missed:

### Traffic

1. Are the right people visiting the site?
2. Which channel created the lead?
3. Is the audience GCC, South Asia, or global?
4. Are visitors serious candidates or casual browsers?
5. Are we targeting working professionals or generic students?

### Landing Page

1. Does the page clearly explain PMP 2026?
2. Is the primary CTA visible?
3. Does the page explain current exam vs updated exam?
4. Does it avoid unsupported affiliation claims?
5. Does it clearly say PM Structure is independent?
6. Does it explain who the pathway is for?
7. Does it show what happens after submitting the form?

### Offer

1. Is the offer name clear?
2. Is the promise specific?
3. Is pricing clear enough?
4. Is regional pricing needed?
5. Does the offer feel urgent because of the PMP 2026 transition?
6. Does the offer solve confusion, structure, and readiness?
7. Is the package scope too vague?

### Lead Form

1. Does the form work?
2. Are submissions reaching the right destination?
3. Is consent wording clear?
4. Are required fields reasonable?
5. Does the form capture exam timeline?
6. Does the form capture country/region?
7. Does the form capture current study status?
8. Does the form capture whether the user wants current or updated PMP route?

### Follow-Up

1. How fast are leads contacted?
2. Is there a clear first response?
3. Are qualification questions being asked?
4. Is the PMP 2026 transition explained clearly?
5. Is there a clear next step?
6. Are objections tracked?
7. Is pricing explained properly?
8. Is the payment or booking process clear?

### Trust

1. Are testimonials real and verified?
2. Are community/member counts verified?
3. Is instructor/founder credibility visible?
4. Are legal disclaimers clear?
5. Are official-body relationships explained safely?
6. Are coming-soon products marked properly?
7. Are pass/job/salary claims avoided?

## Pivot Options

If the PMP 2026 funnel misses targets, choose one pivot path.

### Pivot A — Narrow the Buyer

Focus only on:

```txt
Engineers and project professionals in GCC and South Asia preparing for PMP 2026.
```

Actions:

* Rewrite copy for engineers and technical professionals.
* Use examples from construction, infrastructure, PMO, delivery, operations, and engineering.
* Remove generic certification marketplace wording.
* Make the offer more practical and career-oriented.

---

### Pivot B — Narrow the Offer

Focus only on:

```txt
PMP 2026 Roadmap Diagnostic
```

Actions:

* Sell or offer a low-friction roadmap first.
* Stop pushing full cohort immediately.
* Convert diagnostic users into cohort or mentor-led support.
* Use real questions from diagnostic calls to improve pages.

---

### Pivot C — Shift to Corporate

Focus on:

```txt
Corporate PMP 2026 readiness for teams.
```

Actions:

* Create a corporate one-page proposal.
* Target HR, L&D, PMO, project controls, engineering managers, and training coordinators.
* Offer team readiness mapping.
* Offer cohort pricing.
* Avoid individual price sensitivity.

---

### Pivot D — Shift to Authority Content First

Focus on:

```txt
PMP 2026 answer hub and roadmap lead magnet.
```

Actions:

* Publish direct-answer pages.
* Create a free roadmap download.
* Capture email leads.
* Delay paid cohort until traffic and lead quality improve.
* Keep burn low.

## Decision Rules

Use this table after each checkpoint.

| Situation                                | Decision                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Leads are low and traffic is low         | Fix acquisition and SEO/content distribution.                            |
| Leads are low but traffic is decent      | Fix positioning, CTA, and form.                                          |
| Leads are high but paid learners are low | Fix offer, pricing, trust, and follow-up.                                |
| Sales calls happen but no buyers         | Fix pricing, proof, objection handling, and package clarity.             |
| Buyers ask for official certification    | Fix compliance copy and explain independent support clearly.             |
| Buyers want cheaper options              | Add regional pricing or diagnostic entry offer.                          |
| Buyers do not understand PMP 2026        | Simplify PMP 2026 explanation and add current-vs-updated decision table. |
| Learners buy but do not complete         | Fix onboarding, delivery rhythm, and accountability.                     |
| Learners complete but no testimonials    | Fix testimonial request and permission process.                          |
| No corporate leads                       | Create B2B offer and outreach list.                                      |
