# PM Structure — 90-Day Marketing Schedule

## Purpose

This document defines the first 90 days of PM Structure marketing execution.

The current flagship offer is:

```txt
PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway
```

The marketing goal is not broad brand awareness. The goal is to generate qualified PMP 2026 leads, convert paid learners, collect verified testimonials, and create at least one corporate lead.

This is an internal planning document. Do not publish it on the public website.

---

## Strategic Focus

For the next 90 days, PM Structure should focus on:

1. PMP 2026 exam transition awareness.
2. Current PMP vs updated PMP route decision.
3. PMP eligibility and training-hour clarity.
4. Roadmap requests.
5. GCC and South Asia professionals.
6. Engineers and technical professionals moving into project leadership.
7. Paid learner conversion.
8. Verified testimonial collection.
9. Corporate PMP 2026 readiness lead generation.

Secondary topics such as PRINCE2, Lean Six Sigma, PMI-RMP, membership, and resources should only support comparison, waitlist, or cross-sell positioning.

## Core Audience

Primary audience:

```txt
Working professionals with project, engineering, construction, operations, PMO, planning, delivery, or technical leadership experience who are considering PMP and need structure around the July 2026 exam transition.
```

Primary regions:

* GCC
* South Asia
* professionals targeting GCC/global roles

Priority segments:

1. Engineers moving into project management.
2. Project coordinators and planners.
3. PMO analysts and coordinators.
4. Construction and infrastructure professionals.
5. Operations professionals managing projects.
6. Mid-career professionals with 3–10+ years of experience.
7. Candidates confused about the PMP 2026 exam change.

## Content Production Rules

Every public content piece must follow these rules:

1. Do not claim PM Structure is PMI.
2. Do not claim PM Structure issues PMP.
3. Do not claim PM Structure guarantees exam success.
4. Do not claim guaranteed jobs, promotions, salaries, or visa outcomes.
5. Do not use "35 PDUs" for PMP eligibility.
6. Use "project management education/training" or "training-hour guidance" where relevant.
7. Add source notes where official exam rules are discussed.
8. Use "Last reviewed: 18 June 2026" for PMP 2026 rule pages until updated.
9. Use the primary CTA: "Get My PMP 2026 Roadmap."
10. Mark non-live products as waitlist or coming soon.
