# PM Structure — Funnel Diagnosis Playbook

## Purpose

Use this playbook when PM Structure misses a 30-day, 60-day, or 90-day proof threshold.

The goal is to diagnose the real bottleneck before spending more money or building more features.

This document is internal only.

---

## Diagnosis Flow

Use this order:

1. Traffic
2. Lead capture
3. Lead quality
4. Offer clarity
5. Trust proof
6. Follow-up
7. Pricing
8. Payment flow
9. Delivery
10. Testimonials

Do not jump to paid ads before diagnosing the funnel.

---

## Bottleneck 1 — Low Traffic

Symptoms:

* Few visitors.
* Few form starts.
* Few roadmap requests.
* No serious inquiries.

Likely causes:

* Weak distribution.
* No search visibility yet.
* Not enough direct outreach.
* Content not connected to buyer pain.
* PMP 2026 angle not visible enough.

Actions:

1. Improve PMP 2026 homepage positioning.
2. Publish direct-answer PMP 2026 pages.
3. Share posts around current vs updated PMP exam.
4. Build GCC/South Asia focused content.
5. Do direct outreach to target professionals.
6. Avoid paid ads until the page and tracking are ready.

---

## Bottleneck 2 — Traffic but Few Leads

Symptoms:

* Visitors land on the page.
* Few roadmap requests.
* CTA clicks are low.
* Form starts are low.

Likely causes:

* CTA not clear.
* Offer is too broad.
* PMP 2026 urgency is weak.
* Page does not explain what happens after form submission.
* Trust proof is weak.
* Form is too long or broken.

Actions:

1. Make "Get My PMP 2026 Roadmap" the primary CTA.
2. Add current-vs-updated PMP decision table.
3. Explain who the pathway is for.
4. Explain what happens after submitting the form.
5. Shorten or improve the form.
6. Check mobile layout.
7. Check form delivery.
8. Add independent-platform disclaimer.

---

## Bottleneck 3 — Leads but Few Sales Calls

Symptoms:

* Form submissions exist.
* Leads do not reply.
* Leads do not book calls.
* Leads ask only for free material.

Likely causes:

* Weak lead quality.
* Poor follow-up timing.
* No clear next step.
* No qualification process.
* Offer value is unclear.

Actions:

1. Send follow-up within 24 hours.
2. Ask qualification questions.
3. Explain the PMP 2026 decision point.
4. Offer a roadmap call or diagnostic.
5. Track lead source.
6. Remove spam or low-quality leads from metrics.

---

## Bottleneck 4 — Sales Calls but Few Buyers

Symptoms:

* Serious conversations happen.
* Candidates understand PMP.
* But they do not pay.

Likely causes:

* Pricing mismatch.
* Weak trust proof.
* Package scope unclear.
* No urgency.
* Payment process unclear.
* Candidate doubts legitimacy.
* Offer sounds like generic course content.

Actions:

1. Clarify the package.
2. Add regional pricing logic.
3. Explain independent support clearly.
4. Show what the learner receives.
5. Add safe testimonials when available.
6. Explain payment steps.
7. Offer diagnostic entry package if full cohort is too expensive.
8. Track objections.

---

## Bottleneck 5 — Buyers but Weak Completion

Symptoms:

* Learners pay.
* Learners do not attend or complete.
* Learners stop responding.
* Learners do not follow the plan.

Likely causes:

* Onboarding unclear.
* Delivery rhythm weak.
* Too much self-study dependency.
* No accountability checkpoints.
* No clear next milestone.

Actions:

1. Create onboarding email.
2. Create first-week checklist.
3. Create mock tracking sheet.
4. Schedule readiness checkpoint.
5. Define weekly preparation rhythm.
6. Send reminders.
7. Ask what is blocking progress.

---

## Bottleneck 6 — Completion but No Testimonials

Symptoms:

* Learners complete support.
* Learners benefit.
* No testimonial is collected.

Likely causes:

* No permission process.
* Asking too late.
* Asking too vaguely.
* Learner is unsure what to say.

Actions:

1. Ask immediately after visible value.
2. Use testimonial permission template.
3. Offer full-name, first-name, initials, or anonymous options.
4. Ask specific questions.
5. Avoid pass/job/salary guarantee claims.
6. Store permission record.

## Primary Objection Tracker

Track these objections:

| Objection                       | Meaning                | Response Direction                                                       |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Too expensive                   | Pricing/value mismatch | Explain structure, offer entry diagnostic, regional pricing if approved. |
| Is this official?               | Trust/compliance issue | Explain PM Structure is independent and certification is issued by PMI.  |
| I can study free                | Value clarity issue    | Explain structure, tracking, accountability, and readiness.              |
| I am not eligible               | Qualification issue    | Guide them to verify with PMI and clarify route.                         |
| I am confused about 2026 change | Education issue        | Explain current vs updated exam route.                                   |
| I need employer approval        | B2B opportunity        | Provide corporate/invoice option if available.                           |
| I need guaranteed pass          | Risky expectation      | State no responsible provider can guarantee results.                     |
| I do not have time              | Delivery fit issue     | Offer realistic timeline or defer.                                       |

---

## Final Diagnosis Rule

Do not solve every problem at once.

Identify the main bottleneck first:

```txt
No traffic → fix acquisition.
Traffic but no leads → fix landing page and CTA.
Leads but no calls → fix follow-up.
Calls but no buyers → fix offer, proof, pricing, and objections.
Buyers but no completion → fix delivery.
Completion but no testimonials → fix feedback process.
```
