PMP 2026 Corporate Readiness Cohort

## Target Buyers

- PMOs

- Operations/project delivery teams
- GCC employers
- Teams preparing multiple staff for PMP

## Core Problem

Teams often prepare candidates individually, inconsistently, and too late.

The 2026 PMP transition creates added risk because candidates may use outdated plans, unclear exam-version assumptions, and weak readiness checks.

## Proposed Value

PM Structure can help teams structure PMP 2026 readiness before candidates commit to exam dates.

## Proposed Deliverables

All deliverables require operational approval before public launch.

- Team readiness diagnostic
- PMP 2026 transition briefing
- Candidate eligibility/training-hours review
- Group roadmap
- Weekly readiness checkpoints
- Mock-exam review structure
- Manager progress summary
- Corporate invoice support
- Cohort completion summary
