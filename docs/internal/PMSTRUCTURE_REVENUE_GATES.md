# PM Structure — Internal Revenue Gates

## Purpose

This document defines when PM Structure is allowed to add new monthly costs such as marketing retainers, paid ads, extra tools, automation subscriptions, freelancers, content production, or additional course-pathway expansion.

This is an internal business-control document. Do not publish it on the public website.

PM Structure is currently prioritizing the PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway as the first flagship offer. Until that offer proves real demand and revenue, expansion spending must be controlled.

Related control document: PMSTRUCTURE_KILL_PIVOT_THRESHOLDS.md defines the 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day triggers that must be checked before adding retainers, paid ads, software, contractors, or new certification pathways.

---

## Core Rule

Do not add new recurring costs unless revenue proof exists.

PM Structure should first prove:

* one clear flagship offer,
* one working lead-capture path,
* one repeatable sales script,
* paid learner conversion,
* delivery quality,
* real learner feedback,
* and at least one corporate lead.

Until then, the business should stay lean.

## Revenue Targets

| Revenue Level | Monthly Collected Revenue | Meaning                       | Decision                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------: | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Survival      |              $1,500/month | Basic operating survival      | Keep burn low. No broad expansion.                                                            |
| Healthy       |              $5,000/month | Early proof of working funnel | Consider limited reinvestment only into proven conversion paths.                              |
| Strong Niche  |     $10,000–$20,000/month | Strong niche-education signal | Consider scaling content, cohort delivery, corporate outreach, and selected paid acquisition. |

## Revenue Gate 1 — Below $1,500/month

Status:

```txt
Pre-proof
```

Decision:

Do not add retainers, ads, new freelancers, new certification pathways, or non-critical software.

Allowed spending:

* website stability,
* payment flow,
* analytics,
* lead forms,
* compliance fixes,
* PMP 2026 funnel,
* learner delivery,
* direct sales activity,
* conversion tracking,
* and critical technical fixes.

Not allowed unless explicitly approved:

* paid ad campaigns,
* marketing retainers,
* new automation stack,
* extra designers,
* broad brand campaigns,
* new LMS experiments,
* video production,
* resource-store expansion,
* membership feature expansion,
* PRINCE2 expansion,
* Lean Six Sigma expansion,
* PMI-RMP expansion,
* or large content production before funnel proof.

---

## Revenue Gate 2 — $1,500 to $4,999/month

Status:

```txt
Survival proof
```

Decision:

The business has some proof, but not enough for aggressive expansion.

Allowed spending:

* improve the PMP 2026 funnel,
* improve checkout/payment reliability,
* improve conversion tracking,
* improve onboarding and learner delivery,
* produce content directly linked to PMP 2026 conversion,
* collect and publish verified testimonials,
* create one corporate training proposal,
* and test small manual outreach campaigns.

Conditional spending:

Small paid tests may be allowed only if:

1. The landing page is conversion-ready.
2. Conversion tracking is working.
3. Lead form destination is confirmed.
4. Follow-up process exists.
5. The test has a defined budget cap.
6. Results are reviewed within 7–14 days.
7. Spending can be stopped immediately.

Do not commit to long retainers at this stage unless the owner explicitly approves and records the reason.

## Revenue Gate 3 — $5,000 to $9,999/month

Status:

```txt
Healthy early proof
```

Decision:

The business has early evidence of a working offer. Limited reinvestment is allowed, but only into proven revenue paths.

Allowed spending:

* conversion-focused SEO,
* PMP 2026 content expansion,
* better onboarding,
* cohort delivery support,
* sales follow-up systems,
* analytics/reporting,
* verified testimonial production,
* corporate outreach material,
* limited paid acquisition tests,
* and selective contractor support.

Still avoid:

* broad platform expansion,
* too many certification pages,
* expensive brand campaigns,
* generic social media retainers,
* complex community features,
* and non-revenue software.

Rule:

At this stage, every new cost must answer:

```txt
How does this improve lead quality, conversion rate, learner delivery, or trust proof?
```

If the answer is unclear, delay the cost.

## Retainer Rule

Do not sign a monthly marketing, SEO, ads, automation, content, or agency retainer unless:

1. Current monthly collected revenue is at least **$1,500**, or
2. The retainer directly supports a paid cohort already sold, or
3. The owner explicitly approves it as strategic and records the decision.

Before signing any retainer, record:

| Question                                                           | Answer Required |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| What exact outcome does this retainer create?                      | Required        |
| Does it generate leads, revenue, delivery quality, or trust proof? | Required        |
| What is the monthly cost?                                          | Required        |
| What is the minimum commitment period?                             | Required        |
| Can it be paused within 30 days?                                   | Required        |
| What KPI will prove it is working?                                 | Required        |
| What is the cancellation trigger?                                  | Required        |

---

## Paid Ads Rule

Do not run paid ads unless all of the following are ready:

1. Landing page is focused on PMP 2026 Readiness Pathway.
2. Primary CTA is working.
3. Lead form works.
4. Form submissions go to the correct destination.
5. GA4/GTM conversion events are working.
6. Thank-you page or confirmation event exists.
7. Follow-up message/script exists.
8. Offer price is clear.
9. Refund/terms wording is clear.
10. Campaign has a budget cap.
11. Campaign has a stop-loss rule.

Suggested first paid-ad test only after readiness:

| Item                | Rule                                                                   |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial test budget | Small and capped                                                       |
| Test duration       | 7–14 days                                                              |
| Primary KPI         | Qualified leads                                                        |
| Secondary KPI       | Paid learners                                                          |
| Stop-loss trigger   | No qualified leads or no follow-up response after test period          |
| Scaling trigger     | Qualified leads convert into real sales conversations or paid learners |

## Contractor / Freelancer Rule

Do not add a new contractor unless the task is directly tied to one of these:

* PMP 2026 funnel,
* compliance fix,
* conversion tracking,
* payment/checkout reliability,
* learner delivery,
* verified testimonials,
* corporate proposal,
* sales follow-up,
* or technical stability.

Avoid hiring contractors for:

* general branding,
* broad content production,
* new certification expansion,
* generic social media,
* community growth without active learners,
* and resource-store expansion before paid learner proof.

---

## Software Rule

Do not add new software unless it replaces a manual bottleneck that is already proven.

Before adding software, answer:

1. What manual process is failing?
2. Is the process already generating revenue or qualified leads?
3. Can the same result be achieved manually for 30 more days?
4. Does the tool directly support sales, delivery, tracking, or payment?
5. What software can be cancelled to offset this cost?

If the tool does not improve revenue, delivery, tracking, or trust, delay it.

---

## Expansion Rule

Do not expand into additional certification pathways until PMP 2026 proves demand.

Expansion is allowed only when at least two of the following are true:

1. 20 paid learners reached.
2. 5 verified testimonials with permission collected.
3. 1 corporate lead generated.
4. $5,000/month collected revenue reached.
5. Repeatable sales script created.
6. PMP 2026 delivery process documented.
7. Refund/complaint rate is manageable.
8. Lead source is known and repeatable.

---

## Monthly Revenue Review Questions

Use these questions every month:

1. What was collected revenue this month?
2. How much came from PMP 2026?
3. How many qualified leads were generated?
4. How many paid learners joined?
5. How many sales calls happened?
6. Which channel created the best lead quality?
7. Which cost created measurable revenue?
8. Which cost created no measurable value?
9. Are we adding spend before proof?
10. Which new cost should be rejected or delayed?
11. Which cost should be cancelled?
12. Are we still focused on the flagship offer?
13. Is the next month's spend tied to revenue or vanity?
